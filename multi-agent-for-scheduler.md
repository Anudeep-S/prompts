# P2-SMALL — FAMILY VACATION DECISION ENGINE
## MULTI-SCOUT + MULTI-AGENT TRAVEL COUNCIL

Run a fresh, evidence-driven Council to identify the best family vacation—not merely cheapest airfare, highest Aeroplan CPP, luxury, or fame.

Optimize the whole portfolio: family experience, destination quality, child/toddler practicality, cash, Companion Pass, Aeroplan, TD Aeroplan Visa Infinite Privilege benefits, CIBC Aventura, Costco, lodging, transport, insurance, lounges, PTO, travel time, seasonality, and remaining travel assets.

**OBJECTIVE: MAXIMUM FAMILY EXPERIENCE PER TOTAL PORTFOLIO COST.**

The final recommendation must answer: **Where should this family travel, when, how should each component be paid for, and why is that combination better than the alternatives?**

# 1. EXECUTION ARCHITECTURE

## Stage 1 — Independent Discovery

Run 4 scouts independently. Duplication is acceptable.

**Scout 1A — Pre-Birthday Scout:** ~8 November-2026 concepts exploiting the younger child's under-2/lap-infant opportunity.

**Scout 1B — Seasonal Scout:** ~10–12 Dec-2026–Jun-2027 concepts emphasizing destination/season quality.

**Scout 1C — Value/Asset Scout:** ~8–10 concepts exploiting Companion, Aeroplan, TD, Aventura, Costco, Portland free lodging, or unusual market value.

**Scout 1D — Family Experience Scout:** ~8–10 highly memorable family concepts.

Expected raw pool: **~34–40 concepts.**

## Stage 2 — Consolidation

**Agent 1E** deduplicates, merges near-duplicates, applies hard feasibility constraints, preserves diversity, and avoids premature financial filtering.

Output **EXACTLY 15 candidates.**

## Stage 3 — Parallel Independent Review

The SAME 15 go independently to:

**Agent 2 — Family Advocate:** evaluates family quality and practicality.

**Agent 3A — Market Opportunity Scout:** performs lightweight current-market/economic evaluation.

Neither may see the other's ranking before completing its own.

## Stage 4 — Integration

**Agent 2B** combines both scorecards into **EXACTLY 10 Deal Tournament candidates**:

1. Give automatic advancement consideration to Agent 2 Top 5 and Agent 3A Top 5.
2. Take their union.
3. If <10, fill using combined family-fit + market evidence.
4. If >10, resolve marginal candidates using both scorecards.
5. Preserve ≥1 competitive pre-birthday candidate if one exists.
6. Do not force Portland or another named destination.
7. Introduce no new destinations and do not deeply reprice.

## Stage 5 — Deal Tournament

**Agent 3**

**Pass A:** Build comparable models for ALL 10 covering flights/passenger structure, Companion, Aeroplan, infant, lodging, ground transport, major experiences, approximate all-in CAD, major TD/Aventura opportunities, and major risks. Rank 1–10.

**Pass B:** Deeply optimize approximately the strongest 6–7.

Output:
- **Top 5 Finalists**
- **Reserve #6**
- **Reserve #7**

Reserves remain alive through audit.

## Stages 6–7 — Audit & Repair

**Agent 4 — Skeptical Reviewer:** adversarially audit Top 5. Assume Agent 3 may be wrong.

**Agent 3B — Analyst Repair:** correct valid findings and may promote Reserve #6/#7.

Maximum **TWO audit/repair cycles**.

Round 2 occurs ONLY if Round 1 materially changes pricing, eligibility, ranking, passenger structure, Companion/Aeroplan usage, lodging strategy, material benefit, insurance/payment strategy, OR promotes a reserve.

A promoted reserve must be audited.

After Round 2—or Round 1 if no second cycle is required—**FREEZE evidence and financial metrics**.

Remaining material uncertainty = **[UNKNOWN]** and must affect confidence/status.

Never restart discovery or introduce a new destination. If both reserves fail, allow fewer than five viable finalists rather than inventing replacements.

## Stage 8 — Final Judgment

**Agent 5 — Trip Judge:** use corrected/frozen evidence only. Rank finalists and assign BOOK NOW/WATCH/DROP + confidence.

## Stage 9 — Pre-Birthday Verdict

Compare best trip completed before Dec 1 with best post-birthday trip. Quantify cash saved, Aeroplan preserved, experience/weather/benefit/value differences. Explicitly answer whether the family should travel before the younger child turns 2.

## Stage 10 — Historical Comparison

ONLY after current ranking freezes, compare with previous run. Historical information must never influence discovery.

**Information flow:**

**Scouts → 1E → (Agent 2 || Agent 3A) → 2B → Agent 3 → Agent 4↔3B → Agent 5**

Role boundaries: Scouts discover; 1E consolidates; 2 judges family fit; 3A judges market opportunity; 2B integrates; 3 optimizes; 4 attacks assumptions; 3B repairs/promotes reserves; 5 judges frozen evidence. Do not collapse roles.

# 6. CONSTRAINTS / PRACTICALITY

## Level 1 — Hard/Near-Hard

May eliminate:
- outside travel window
- cannot reasonably fit 4–6 nights
- impossible ticketing/geography
- routinely extreme journey
- major seasonal closure destroying concept
- clearly unsuitable family trip

## Level 2 — Strong Preferences

Influence ranking:
- nonstop
- sensible flight times
- limited driving
- stroller practicality
- value lodging
- simple logistics
- one accommodation base
- good weather

## Level 3 — Optimization Only

Improve economics but should not control discovery:
- Companion
- lap infant
- Aeroplan
- TD benefits
- Fourth Night/Day
- Aventura
- Costco
- lounges/status

Failure to use every Level-3 benefit is NOT grounds to reject an excellent vacation.

### Flight Boundaries

Nonstop preferred.

- ≤6h nonstop: ideal
- 6–7h: acceptable for exceptional trip
- one-stop ≤8h: preferred
- 8–10h: strong penalty
- >10h: normally reject
- preferred maximum: 1 connection
- avoid airport changes

Prefer departure ~7am–7pm; penalize before ~6am.

Prefer arrival before ~9pm; strongly penalize after ~11pm.

Avoid red-eyes unless compelling.

### Driving

≤2h/day preferred.

One 3–4h scenic transfer acceptable.

Repeated 3+h driving days receive strong penalty.

### PTO

PTO is a tie-breaker.

Report calendar duration, weekdays away, applicable holidays, and net PTO.

# 18. CORE PRINCIPLES

Discovery = **BROAD AND DIVERSE.**

Family review = **EXPERIENCE-CENTRIC.**

Market review = **INDEPENDENT AND PRICE-AWARE.**

Shortlisting = **MULTI-SIGNAL.**

Deal analysis = **COMPARABLE FIRST, PRECISE SECOND.**

Audit = **ADVERSARIAL.**

Repair = **CONTROLLED.**

Final judgment = **HOLISTIC.**

Remember:

**Experiences > luxury accommodation.**

**Simple logistics > unnecessary complexity.**

**True all-in price > advertised price.**

**Verified benefit > assumed benefit.**

**A benefit should improve a vacation; a vacation should not exist merely to use a benefit.**

**Preserving Aeroplan can be valuable; using Aeroplan can also be valuable when redemption is strong.**

**Pre-Dec-1 lap-infant travel is a genuine one-time opportunity—not an automatic winner.**

**A safe, clean, practical value stay plus unforgettable experiences can beat a luxury hotel.**

**Do not take a mediocre vacation solely to save the toddler's airline seat.**

Final objective:

**Create the most memorable, practical and financially intelligent family vacation possible using the family's cash, points, travel benefits and limited vacation time.**
