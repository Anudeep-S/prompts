Preserve P1 as the baseline architecture.

**P2**
This version.

P2 retains the family profile, travel assets, constraints, value philosophy and adversarial verification framework from P1 but replaces the oversized Agent 1 with multiple independent discovery scouts.

> **Discover broadly first. Apply constraints progressively. Price intelligently. Verify aggressively. Decide only after evidence is frozen.**

Do NOT force every constraint into every discovery agent.

# 18. CONSTRAINT CLASSIFICATION

P2 separates constraints into THREE levels.

This distinction is mandatory.

## LEVEL 1 — HARD / NEAR-HARD CONSTRAINTS

These can eliminate a candidate.

Examples:

- Outside travel window
- Cannot reasonably fit 4–6 nights
- Impossible airline/ticketing structure
- Fundamentally incompatible geography
- Routinely extreme travel time
- Major seasonal closure destroying the concept
- Clearly unsuitable for the family

## LEVEL 2 — STRONG PREFERENCES

These influence ranking but should not automatically kill discovery.

Examples:

- Nonstop flight
- ≤6-hour flight
- Limited driving
- Good flight times
- Stroller practicality
- Value accommodation
- Simple logistics
- One accommodation base
- Good weather

## LEVEL 3 — OPTIMIZATION OPPORTUNITIES

These should generally IMPROVE a candidate rather than determine whether it is discovered.

Examples:

- Companion Pass
- Lap infant
- Aeroplan
- 15% TD benefit
- Fourth Night Free
- Fourth Day Free
- Aventura
- Costco
- Lounge
- Rental status

Failure to exploit every Level-3 benefit is NOT grounds for rejecting an otherwise excellent vacation.
