# FAMILY VACATION DECISION ENGINE
## MULTI-SCOUT + MULTI-AGENT TRAVEL COUNCIL

Run a fresh, evidence-driven Multi-Agent Family Travel Council.

The purpose is to identify the best family vacation for this specific family—not simply the cheapest airfare, highest Aeroplan CPP, most luxurious accommodation, or most famous destination.

The Council must optimize the COMPLETE family travel portfolio:

- Family experience
- Destination quality
- Older-child enjoyment
- Toddler practicality
- Cash expenditure
- Air Canada Annual Worldwide Companion Pass
- Aeroplan
- TD Aeroplan Visa Infinite Privilege benefits
- CIBC Aventura
- Costco
- Accommodation
- Ground transportation
- Travel insurance
- Airport/lounging benefits
- PTO
- Travel time
- Seasonality
- Remaining travel assets after the trip

The overall objective is:

> **MAXIMUM FAMILY EXPERIENCE PER TOTAL PORTFOLIO COST.**

The final recommendation must answer:

> **Where should this family travel, when should they travel, how should they pay for each component, and why is that combination better than the alternatives?**

# 1. EXECUTION ARCHITECTURE

This Council uses a staged decision tournament.

Each stage has a distinct responsibility.

Do NOT collapse stages together.

Do NOT allow financial optimization to prematurely restrict destination discovery.

## STAGE 1 — INDEPENDENT DISCOVERY

Run FOUR scouts with intentionally different perspectives.

### Scout 1A — Pre-Birthday Opportunity Scout

Search specifically for excellent November 2026 trips that can exploit the younger child's pre-second-birthday lap-infant opportunity.

Target:

**~8 concepts**

### Scout 1B — Seasonal Destination Scout

Search Dec 2026–Jun 2027 primarily for excellent seasonality, weather, attractions and destination timing.

Target:

**~10–12 concepts**

### Scout 1C — Value & Travel-Asset Scout

Search for opportunities created by Companion Pass, Aeroplan, TD benefits, Aventura, Costco, Portland free lodging and unusual travel economics.

### Scout 1D — Family Experience Scout

Search primarily for highly memorable trips for two adults, an older child and a toddler.

**~8–10 concepts**

Expected raw discovery:

**~34–40 concepts**

Duplication between scouts is acceptable.

# 20. CONSTRAINT CLASSIFICATION

Use THREE levels.

## LEVEL 1 — HARD / NEAR-HARD

May eliminate:

- Outside travel window
- Cannot reasonably fit 4–6 nights
- Impossible ticketing
- Fundamentally incompatible geography
- Routinely extreme travel time
- Major seasonal closure destroying core concept
- Clearly unsuitable for family

## LEVEL 2 — STRONG PREFERENCES

Influence ranking:

- Nonstop
- ≤6-hour flight
- Limited driving
- Good flight times
- Stroller practicality
- Value accommodation
- Simple logistics
- One accommodation base
- Good weather

## LEVEL 3 — OPTIMIZATION OPPORTUNITIES

Improve economics but should not control discovery:

- Companion
- Lap infant
- Aeroplan
- TD reward benefits
- Fourth Night
- Fourth Day
- Aventura
- Costco
- Lounge
- Rental status

Failure to use every Level-3 benefit is NOT grounds for rejecting an excellent vacation.

# 67. CORE DECISION PRINCIPLES

Discovery should be:

**BROAD AND DIVERSE.**

Family evaluation should be:

**EXPERIENCE-CENTRIC.**

Market evaluation should be:

**INDEPENDENT AND PRICE-AWARE.**

Shortlisting should be:

**MULTI-SIGNAL.**

Deal analysis should be:

**COMPARABLE FIRST, PRECISE SECOND.**

Audit should be:

**ADVERSARIAL.**

Repair should be:

**CONTROLLED.**

Final judgment should be:

**HOLISTIC.**

Remember:

**Experiences > luxury accommodation.**

**Simple logistics > unnecessary complexity.**

**True all-in price > advertised price.**

**Verified benefit > assumed benefit.**

**A benefit should improve a vacation; a vacation should not exist merely to use a benefit.**

**Preserving Aeroplan can be valuable.**

**Using Aeroplan can be valuable when redemption is strong.**

**Pre-Dec-1 lap-infant travel is a genuine one-time opportunity—not an automatic winner.**

**A safe, clean, practical value stay plus unforgettable experiences can beat a luxury hotel.**

**Do not take a mediocre vacation solely to save the toddler's airline seat.**

The final objective remains:

> **Create the most memorable, practical and financially intelligent family vacation possible using the family's cash, points, travel benefits and limited vacation time.**
